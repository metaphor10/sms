Program

Ariel Borochov sends recurring account and order text messages to users who opt in. Messages include order status, appointment reminders, and account alerts. This program does not send promotional marketing, loan offers, or age-gated content.

Opting in

You opt in by submitting the form at the SMS signup page, including your mobile number and checking the consent box, or by texting START. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Cost and frequency

Message frequency varies based on account activity, typically 2 to 8 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to cancel. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help.

Privacy

Mobile numbers are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. See the Privacy Policy.
